# Resolved spikes

Evidence for each is in `docs/research/`. Statements are limited to what the samples show.

* **S1. `MI_QFIIS` coverage.** On four dates (2015-06-01, 2018-06-01, 2022-06-01, 2025-06-02) none of 858, 915, 964 and 1,041 common-stock codes lacked a `MI_QFIIS` row. An earlier comparison of 915 `MI_QFIIS` rows with 951 `MI_INDEX` rows used dates a year apart and was an artefact. FinMind `TaiwanStockShareholding` stays the cross-check only.
* **S2. Limit-price date meaning.** A `TWT84U` row dated D gives the limits for D (its `收盤價` is the previous trading day's close). On 2020-06-15 this held for 1,094 of the 1,094 stocks that had a row (the file has 1,116 rows). TPEx `otc` next-day limit columns in file D apply to the next trading day with a non-empty file, verified on three consecutive pairs of 2025 files only. About 98 TPEx rows carry a `9999.95`/`0.01` no-limit sentinel (bond ETFs), stored as null. Publication times are unverified.
* **S3. Endpoint ranges.** `TWTAWU` works for 2015 (2 rows, both depositary receipts). `TWT49U` returned 11,863 rows for 2015-06-01 to 2026-11-13 in one call, with no truncation marker (inferred from counts and every year present). It takes 9 to over 60 seconds, so the client timeout is at least 150 seconds, or calls are chunked by year. TPEx `revivt` returns 21 to 29 rows per year for 2016 to 2021.
* **S4. Common-stock rule.** On 11 TWSE dates and 10 distinct TPEx years, the regex `^[1-9][0-9]{3}$` plus a name rule (trailing `*` removed, not ending `-DR`) had no leaks and no misses among ordinary common stocks. The `-DR` clause matters on TWSE (4 to 7 four-digit DR codes pass the regex). QA re-tests the rule on one date per year.
* **S7. FinMind call limits.** An 11-year, one-stock call returned 13,346 institutional flow rows, 17,960 securities lending rows and 63,914 `TaiwanFuturesDaily` rows for `TX`, with no round-number count that suggests a cap. Nine datasets were tested this way. `TaiwanStockFinancialStatements`, `TaiwanStockBalanceSheet`, `TaiwanStockCashFlowsStatement` and `TaiwanStockDividend` were only existence-checked, and the first three have many rows per quarter, so they are the likeliest to hit a cap (open, S12).
* **S8. Corporate-action coverage.** `TWT49U` covers ETFs, including every 0050 dividend from 2015 to 2026. None of the four feeds checked (`TWT49U`, `TWTAUU`, `exDailyQ`, `revivt`) carries stock splits or par-value changes, for the windows searched. The `TWT49U` wording about split capital reductions probably refers to demerger reductions, so that is a possible further gap. FinMind prices show 0050 falling from 188.65 (2025-06-10) to 47.57 (2025-06-18), a ratio of about 4, with no rows on 2025-06-11 to 2025-06-17. That is consistent with a 4-for-1 split after a halt. The exact ratio and reference price come from the exchange announcement (open, S11).
* **S9. Era boundaries and cross-checks.** `MI_INDEX` has the same per-stock field list on seven 2015 dates and ten 2016 to 2025 dates. TPEx `otc` has three eras: 15 fields until 2020-04-29, 17 fields with `(千股)` from 2020-04-30, and 17 fields with `(張數)` from 2025-01-10 (three 2015 dates match the 2016 layout). FinMind closes for 2330 and 6488 have no more than 2 decimals and matched the exchange closes in 20 of 20 pairs (ten dates each, 2016 to 2025). The sample is small, so check 4 still runs.
