# Hypothesis card H1: drift after monthly revenue surprises

Committed before any test (plan section 10.3). Status: not yet tested.

*   **Claim:** Among liquid Taiwan stocks, those whose latest monthly revenue shows the strongest year-on-year growth outperform the others over the following month, after costs.
*   **Mechanism:** Monthly revenue is published by the 10th of the following month and is the first hard number on quarterly earnings. Retail and slow institutional investors under-react to it and correct over the next weeks. The other side of the trade is investors who sell or ignore a strong print and buy a weak one late. They keep losing because revenue is public and cheap to read, but sorting thousands of names every month is work that small holders do not do.
*   **Data and `available_at`:** `monthly_revenue` (`available_at` = the 10th of the following month at 18:00, or the next trading day, from `avail_revenue`), `universe_membership` (month-end ranked universe), `prices_raw` and adjusted prices. The signal is computed only from rows whose `available_at` is on or before the decision time. Decision time is 18:00 on the first trading day on or after the 10th. Execution is the next trading day open.
*   **Signal (the only free parameters, fixed here):** `yoy = revenue(month m) / revenue(month m-12) - 1` from the same stock's rows; require both months present. Score = `yoy` rank within the universe (percentile). Long the top decile by score, equal weight, at most 10% per name (if fewer than 10 names qualify, hold cash for the rest). Rebalance once a month on the decision date above. No short leg (retail shorting in Taiwan is constrained and costly).
*   **Holding period and turnover:** about 20 trading days, monthly rebalance. Expected one-way turnover 40% to 60% a month.
*   **Predicted effect and falsification:** Predicted: top-decile net excess return over 0050 of about 0.5% to 1.0% a month. This card is **falsified** if, on the validation window (2022-01 to 2024-06), net Sharpe after costs is below 0.5, or net mean excess return over 0050 is not above zero, or the top decile does not beat the median stock on gross monthly return. It is **not** adjusted after seeing results. Changes become a new card.
*   **Prior evidence:** Post-earnings-announcement drift and revenue-surprise drift are documented in several markets (Bernard and Thomas, 1989; revenue growth surprises in Taiwan studies). Not verified in this project.
*   **Test budget:** the first run of this card uses 1 of the cycle's 10 backtests. A parameter sweep over the top fraction, rebalance day or ranking rule counts one backtest per setting.
