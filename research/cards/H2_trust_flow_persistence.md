# Hypothesis card H2: persistence in investment-trust net buying

**Status: draft, pre-charter.** Not reviewed, not ranked, not committed, not tested. Predicted numbers are a-priori guesses, not calibrated on any data.

*   **Claim:** Among universe stocks, those with the heaviest cumulative net buying by investment trusts (domestic funds) over the last 10 trading days outperform the universe over the next 20 trading days, after costs.
*   **Mechanism:** Trusts are large relative to mid-cap float and build positions over days or weeks, so their buying keeps pushing price after the first report. They also have a quarter-end performance incentive that favours holding recent winners into reporting dates. The other side is sellers who see a stock "run" and sell into continuing demand, and short-horizon traders who fade the move. Caveat: part of the effect may simply be flows chasing past returns (momentum), so the card is only interesting if it adds to a plain price-momentum control.
*   **Data and `available_at`:** `TaiwanStockInstitutionalInvestorsBuySell` (investment-trust column), `universe_membership`, shares issued (for scaling), `prices_raw` and adjusted prices. Flows for day t are published after the close, so `available_at` is the next trading day (plan section 4). Decision time is 18:00 on the first day the 10th flow day is available; execution is the next trading day open.
*   **Signal (the only free parameters, fixed here):** `flow = sum of trust net buy shares over days t-9..t / shares issued`. Score = percentile rank in the universe. Long the top quintile (about 40 names), equal weight, cap 5% per name. Rebalance every 10 trading days. No short leg.
*   **Holding period and turnover:** 10 trading days between rebalances, holding about 20 days on average because of overlap. Expected one-way turnover 50% to 70% per rebalance. Cost drag is the main risk.
*   **Predicted effect and falsification:** Predicted net excess return over 0050 of 0.2% to 0.6% per month. **Falsified** on the validation window if net Sharpe after costs is below 0.5, net mean excess return over 0050 is not above zero, or the top quintile does not beat the median stock on gross 20-day return. A diagnostic, not part of the falsification rule: the same score residualised on trailing 60-day return, to see whether flow adds to momentum.
*   **Prior evidence:** Foreign investors in Taiwan behave as momentum traders and their trades predict returns for large or low book-to-market stocks (Lai, Lou and Shiu, 2008, per an abstract only). Evidence on which investor type is informed is mixed (Hsieh and He, 2014; Chiu, Lee and Wang, 2014; both on index options). Trust-specific stock-level evidence not found. Not verified in this project.
*   **Dataset status:** In plan P1 (`TaiwanStockInstitutionalInvestorsBuySell`). Whether collected and QA-passed: unknown to the author of this card (data not read). Shares issued comes from `TaiwanStockShareholding`, also P1.
*   **Cluster:** Neighbours: H1 (both are "information diffuses slowly"), price momentum. Expect correlation with momentum; the reviewer should ask for the residual test.
*   **Open questions for review:** Is a 10-day window too long given next-day availability? Does foreign flow (the larger player) deserve its own card, or does it dilute the claim? Is quarter-end window dressing a calendar effect that makes a monthly-uniform rebalance the wrong design?
